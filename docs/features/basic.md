# Basic Functionality

Spine brings supported Spine 4.1 characters to SpriteKit on iOS, macOS, tvOS and watchOS.

- Load characters, their images, skins and animations.
- Play animations once, in sequence or repeatedly, with playback speed and pause controls.
- Animate bone movement, rotation, scale, attachment visibility and drawing order.
- Switch skins, replace images and adjust character appearance.
- Receive animation events and completion notifications.
- Attach game objects to bones and named attachment points.
- Use static collision shapes with SpriteKit physics.

Characters remain ordinary SpriteKit scene objects and keep independent animation and appearance state.
